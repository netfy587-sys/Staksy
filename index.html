Your 30 days with Staksy 🗓️

A simple plan that works for almost any skill, from coding to cooking to a new language.

  1. Days 1 to 3: Choose and define

    Pick one skill and write one clear goal, such as "hold a five-minute conversation" or "build a small website".

  2. Week 1: Learn the basics

    Study the core ideas in short sessions. Focus on understanding, not on collecting resources.

  3. Week 2: Practise actively

    Close the tutorial and try things yourself. Testing what you remember teaches more than re-reading.

  4. Week 3: Build something

    Make a small real project. Mistakes here are the fastest teachers.

  5. Week 4: Review and share

    Revisit weak spots, show your work to others and set the next goal.

📝 Quick takeaways

  • Short daily sessions beat long irregular ones.
  • Test yourself instead of only re-reading.
  • Build small projects early.
  • Sleep well: rest helps memory settle.
  • Progress you can see keeps you going.

How the brain actually learns new skills

Learning feels slow when we use methods that only feel productive. Research on memory points to a few habits that work better, and all of them are easy to start.

Active recall

Instead of reading a page again, close it and try to write down what you remember. The small effort of retrieving an idea strengthens it far more than passive review.

Spaced repetition

Review material at growing intervals: the next day, then a few days later, then a week. Spacing out practice helps knowledge last longer than cramming.

Deliberate practice

Work slightly beyond your comfort level, get feedback, then repeat. Ten minutes on the exact thing you find hard is worth more than an hour repeating what you already know.

Mixing it up

Alternating related topics, such as different kinds of problems, feels harder but builds the skill of choosing the right approach.

🧠 Remember

Struggling a little is a sign you are learning. If everything feels easy, raise the difficulty slightly.
